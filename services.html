Our Programs

Whether you're ready to adopt, can open your home for a few weeks, or have a few hours to give, there's a place for you in our rescue family.

Adoption Process

  1. Browse

    Meet our adoptable animals online or at a weekend adoption event.

  2. Apply

    Complete a short adoption application so we can learn about your home and lifestyle.

  3. Meet and greet

    Spend time with the animal to make sure it's a good match for everyone, including current pets.

  4. Home check

    A volunteer visits, or video-calls, to help you prepare a safe space.

  5. Welcome home

    Sign the adoption agreement, pay the adoption fee, and start your new life together.

* Adoption fees range from $25 to $250 and include spay or neuter surgery, vaccinations and a microchip.

Foster Program

Foster homes are the heart of our rescue. When you foster, you give an animal a safe place to recover, gain confidence and get ready for adoption, and you free up space so we can save another life.

  • What foster families provide

    • A safe, loving temporary home, usually for two to eight weeks
    • Daily care, attention and gentle socialization
    • Transportation to vet visits and adoption events, when possible

  • What we provide

    • All food, supplies and veterinary care at no cost
    • Training and a dedicated foster coordinator
    • 24/7 support for emergencies

No experience? No problem. We match fosters with animals that fit their home and comfort level.

Volunteer Roles

Animal care assistant
Help feed, clean, walk and socialize animals at our partner facilities. Shifts are about 2-3 hours.
Adoption event helper
Greet visitors, answer questions and help match families with pets at weekend events around the metro.
Transport driver
Drive animals to vet appointments, foster homes and adoption events. A valid driver's license and a reliable vehicle are required.
Fundraising and outreach
Plan events, staff community booths and help spread the word on social media.

Ready to get involved? Fill out our volunteer interest form and we'll be in touch within a few days.
