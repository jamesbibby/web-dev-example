Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a volunteer-powered nonprofit connecting homeless pets with caring families throughout Minneapolis, St. Paul and the surrounding metro.

Mission Statement

We rescue, rehabilitate and rehome animals in need, and we build a community where every pet is safe, healthy and loved. Through adoption, fostering and education, we work toward a future where no adoptable animal is left without a home.

Our Impact (2025)

  • 1,240 animals adopted into permanent homes
  • 310 active foster families
  • 18,500 volunteer hours contributed
  • 96% of rescued animals placed in homes
  • 42 community partners supporting our work

How You Can Help

  • Adopt: Open your home to a pet who's ready for a fresh start. Learn about our adoption process.
  • Foster: Give an animal a safe, temporary place to heal and grow while they wait for their family. Explore the foster program.
  • Volunteer: From animal care to adoption events, there's a role for every schedule and skill. See volunteer roles.
