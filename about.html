About Us

We're neighbors helping neighbors, two-legged and four-legged alike.

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue began in 2014 when a small group of friends in South Minneapolis started fostering stray cats out of their apartments. Word spread quickly. Within a year, a dozen foster homes were caring for animals that had nowhere else to go.

Today we're a volunteer-powered nonprofit with more than 300 foster families across Minneapolis, St. Paul and the surrounding suburbs. We don't operate a traditional shelter. Instead, every animal in our care lives in a real home, where it gets one-on-one attention while it waits for its forever family.

Our goal hasn't changed since day one: every adoptable animal deserves a safe place to land and a second chance at a happy life.

Our Core Values

Compassion
Every animal is treated with patience, dignity and love, whatever its age, breed or history.
Transparency
We're open about how we spend every donated dollar and how every animal in our care is doing.
Community
Rescue is a team effort. We succeed because neighbors, partners and volunteers show up together.
Responsibility
We match pets and people thoughtfully, so adoptions last a lifetime.
Hope
No animal is a lost cause. We believe in second chances.
